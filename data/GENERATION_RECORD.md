# Data provenance and processing record

No active image was generated by AI. The former two-image AI technical pack is stored only under `outputs/retired-ai-pack/` as historical POC evidence and is not a classroom input.

## Selection

- two consented full-body front-view crops from two HSRD-100 scan participants;
- five real-vehicle frames from five visibly distinct recording groups in Driver Risk Behavior Dataset;
- three frames from three distinct HADRIAN participant IDs;
- two guided and eight independent images;
- natural pose cases include nominal driving, relaxed/off-wheel hands, oblique pose, forward lean, handheld-object interaction and hand-to-face occlusion;
- deterministic sensor cases include low light, glare and partial obstruction.

## Processing

`scripts/build-data-pack.py` verifies source archive/member or extracted-frame hashes, crops real-vehicle frames to the driver ROI, applies opaque face masks where the source mask is absent, applies only declared pixel transforms and re-encodes RGB JPEG without EXIF/ICC/XMP. It never synthesizes a person or hallucinates content.

The source dataset's behaviour class is retained only in the manifest as `provenance-only`; it is not a student label and cannot establish distraction, drowsiness or intent from a still image.

Run:

```bash
python3 scripts/audit-data-pack.py
```

The audit fails unless the active pack has exactly 10 unique participant codes, all three approved sources, a 2/8 evidence-lane split, full-17 calibration coverage, cabin privacy rules, setup diversity, expected licences and byte-exact hashes.
